Vision Document & Architecture

Restaurant Management System

"To build a robust, user-friendly, and cost-effective digital solution that streamlines restaurant operations from order taking to final billing."

Lead Front-End Architect: Fasiha Muzzammil (fasihacodes)
Explore Project Scope

Why Build This System?

The Current Problems

  • ❌ Manual paper-based order taking causes errors.
  • ❌ Miscommunication between waiters and kitchen staff.
  • ❌ Slow billing process during rush hours.
  • ❌ Difficulty in tracking daily sales and inventory.

Core Objectives

  • ✅ Automate order processing for faster service.
  • ✅ Provide 100% accurate, calculated bills.
  • ✅ Enable real-time communication with the kitchen.
  • ✅ Maintain a reliable digital record of operations.

Stakeholders & Role-Based Access (RBAC)

Requirements gathered through structured staff interviews by Fasiha Muzzammil.

👨‍💼

Owner / Manager

Full Admin Access. Focus on reports & menu updates.

🧑‍🍳

Kitchen Staff

Restricted Access. Focus on incoming live orders.

📝

Order Taker

Restricted Access. Focus on fast table data entry.

💰

Cashier

Billing Access. Focus on payment processing.

Project Scope & Iterations

First Iteration

Core Functional Scope

1. Order Management

Digital table orders synced directly to the kitchen display.

2. Menu Management

Digital catalog with real-time item pricing and status updates.

3. Billing & Payments

Automated invoice generation and payment logging.

Out of Boundary

What We Are NOT Building

  • Third-party delivery app integrations (Foodpanda)
  • Employee Payroll & Salary Management
  • Multi-branch cloud database synchronization
  • Online customer portal table reservations

Expected Business Impact

Cost & Wastage Control

Accurate inventory tracking reduces food wastage and operational overhead.

Revenue Growth

Faster table turnaround and error-free billing directly increase net profits.

Better Decisions

Management receives clear analytics on best-selling items and peak hours.
